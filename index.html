Essay Format

Every ALAC essay problem in these modules asks you to structure your answer in four parts:

AAnswer: Categorical, direct affirmative or negative reply.
LLegal Basis: Specific codal provision, doctrine, or statute.
AApplication: Direct intertwining of facts with elements of law.
CConclusion: Final disposition based on applied rules.
